2.5.2.114041 for Minecraft 1.14.4

This brings the released 1.13.2 build across to Forge 28, using OreSpawn 4.1.7.114041. The mineral IDs, ore depths and deposit shapes stay the same, and saved ore settings carry forward.

The ores keep their earlier drops, Fortune and Silk Touch behaviour, and work with Base Metals or Modern Metals crackhammers. Lithium and silicon can also be smelted in a blast furnace. Molten fluids use Forge's native renderer, and the ingot trades use the new villager professions.

The minerals now join both hammer mining tags, so breaking an ore uses the hammer's proper speed. Local test launches pin the fixed Base Metals and Modern Metals jars by hash, including the ore overlay fix.

The old numeric-world conversion is adapted for 1.14.4. Keep the full save and its baseminerals-legacy-registry.dat file together, and test an upgrade on a copy. The older material settings screens and Tinkers integration are still unavailable.

2.5.2.113021 for Minecraft 1.13.2

This fixes the ore drops to match 1.10 to 1.12. Lithium drops one ore block; the other six ores drop 2–4 powders. Fortune increases the maximum, and Silk Touch returns the ore block.

The missing lithium and silicon smelting recipes are back, along with block, ingot and nugget crushing. This also restores the earlier mining hardness, tool requirements, blast resistance, storage-block beacon support, molten-fluid light and smith ingot trades.

Eclipse launches now work when the project path contains spaces.

2.5.1.113021 for Minecraft 1.13.2

- Bring the eight minerals and seven ore deposits across to Forge 25, without MMDLib.
- Convert old metadata to named block states and keep saved mineral blocks, fluids and buckets when upgrading a copied world.
- Share crushing recipes with Base Metals 3.0.3, or Modern Metals when Base Metals is absent.
- Keep KiriCattus's ore overlays on the active stone texture and use OreSpawn's native provider.
- Use OreSpawn 4.1.7 for the latest world generation and saved-profile fixes.
- Fix missing fluids and startup hangs with other fluid-adding mods during parallel loading.
- The old material settings screens and Tinkers integration are not available on 1.13.2. Ore generation is configured through OreSpawn.

2.5.1.112021 for 1.12.2

This brings the 1.10 and 1.11 updates across to 1.12.2. Use Forge 14.23.5.2859 with MMDLib 1.0.0 RC3 and OreSpawn 4.1.6.112021. Base Metals is optional. I checked that its hammer works on the ores with Base Metals 2.6.2.112021 installed.

The seven ores use the same depths and deposit shapes as the 1.10 and 1.11 updates, with KiriCattus's overlays on vanilla stone. Your saved ore settings and existing chunks carry forward. If you're moving an OreSpawn 1 world, import its settings on 1.10.2 or 1.11.2 first and bring the saved profile with you.

You can change material and fluid settings from Forge Mods or OreSpawn Mods. Restart Minecraft to apply them. Disabled materials switch off their recipes, and lithium and silicon still work with Tinkers' Construct.
