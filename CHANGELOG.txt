2.5.1.110021 for 1.10.2

This brings the 1.10.2 build up to date on Forge 12.18.3.2511. It needs Base Metals 2.6.0.110021 and OreSpawn 4.1.6.110021.

The seven ores have new depths and deposit shapes, and now use KiriCattus's overlays on vanilla stone. Saved ore settings and existing chunks are kept. OreSpawn now preserves the imported lithium rule when you move an old OS1 world and can remove an untouched duplicate left by an earlier version.

Base Electric shares sulfur and lithium placement with Base Minerals. In a pack with both Base Electric and Mineralogy, Mineralogy places sulfur and Base Minerals places lithium.

Material settings are available from Forge Mods and OreSpawn Mods and take effect after a restart. This update also fixes disabled material recipes and achievements, charcoal ownership, and molten material textures.

The old Electric Advantage 2.2.0 and Power Advantage 2.3.0 releases are incompatible with this Base Metals build.
